 Pharma Care Healthcare Ltd. — Pharmaceutical Recall & Risk Analytics Dashboard

 📊 Project Overview

Pharma Care Healthcare Ltd. is a Power BI analytics project developed to analyze pharmaceutical product recalls and understand their inventory, financial, operational, and business impact.

The project transforms raw pharmaceutical data into an interactive 4-page Power BI dashboard, enabling users to analyze recalled products and batches, inventory losses, financial liabilities, warehouse activity, temperature excursions, and overall recall costs.

---

Business Problem :

Pharmaceutical recalls can result in significant financial losses, inventory disruptions, customer refunds, replacement costs, and operational risks.

The objective of this project was to build a centralized analytics solution that helps stakeholders:

* Monitor pharmaceutical recall activity
* Identify affected products and batches
* Measure inventory losses
* Estimate financial liabilities
* Analyze replacement costs
* Identify operational and temperature-related risks
* Analyze warehouse-level activity
* Identify reconciliation gaps
* Understand the overall estimated cost of recall events

📌 Dashboard Pages

1️⃣ Recall Overview

The first page provides a high-level overview of pharmaceutical recall activity.

 Key analysis:

* Total recall events
* Affected products
* Affected batches
* Total inventory impacted
* Estimated recall cost
* Recall status
* Product/batch distribution
* Overall recall trends

 Purpose

Provides stakeholders with a quick understanding of the overall recall situation and its scale.

---

2️⃣ Product & Batch Analysis

This page focuses on identifying products and batches affected by recalls.

Key analysis:

* Product-level recall analysis
* Batch-level analysis
* Affected quantities
* Recall frequency
* Product distribution
* Batch status
* Product and batch drill-downs

 Purpose

Helps identify which products and batches are contributing to recall activity and allows users to investigate individual recall cases.

---

3️⃣ Inventory & Financial Impact

This page evaluates the financial consequences of pharmaceutical recalls.

Key metrics:

* Inventory Loss
* Refund Liability
* Replacement Cost
* Total Estimated Recall Cost
* Financial Impact
* Affected Inventory

Example calculations

Inventory Loss

Measures the financial impact associated with recalled inventory.

Refund Liability

Estimates the amount associated with refunds for affected products.

Replacement Cost

Calculates the estimated cost of replacing recalled products.

Total Estimated Recall Cost

Combines relevant recall-related financial components to provide an overall estimate of recall exposure.

# Purpose

Provides visibility into the financial consequences of recall events and helps stakeholders understand the overall economic impact.

---

 4️⃣ Operational Risk Analysis

The fourth page focuses on operational factors associated with pharmaceutical recalls.

 Key analysis:

* Temperature excursion alerts
* Warehouse activity
* Warehouse dispatches
* Operational risk indicators
* Affected inventory
* Recall-related operational patterns
* Reconciliation gaps

# Purpose

Helps identify operational conditions that may be associated with product quality issues and recall events.
